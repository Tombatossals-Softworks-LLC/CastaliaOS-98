Tango Icon Library
==================

Used by CastaliaOS 98 PE as the default desktop/toolbar/Quick Launch icon pack
(assets/icons/tango/, installed to C:\CASTALIA\ICONS\TANGO). The icons were
downsized to the CastaliaOS keyed-BMP convention with tools/png2bmp.py; the
artwork is unmodified in content.

Source (rasterized PNGs, flattened naming):
  https://github.com/nigeltao/tango-icon-library-pngs
Upstream project:
  http://tango.freedesktop.org/Tango_Icon_Library
Original tarball the mirror copied from:
  http://tango.freedesktop.org/releases/tango-icon-theme-0.8.90.tar.gz
